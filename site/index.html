The road to February

The NFL season runs from late summer to mid-winter, and every game matters because there are so few of them.

Then & Now

A century of football

1920

It starts in Canton

The American Professional Football Association forms in Canton, Ohio. Two years later it renames itself the National Football League.

1967

The first Super Bowl

The NFL champion meets the rival AFL champion in a game that would grow into the biggest event in American sports.

1970

The merger

The NFL and AFL officially combine, creating the AFC and NFC conferences that still exist today.
